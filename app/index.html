The road to Leonida Legend

Seven stages, in the order this companion recommends playing them. The story first, with the save discipline that protects the one confirmed missable; the sweeps afterwards, region by region, so no count becomes a single end-game slog.

Missables & warnings

One trophy is confirmed missable at launch. Three more carry a caution: their methods are unconfirmed or one-chance, and this game has no mission replay — so the real protection is a manual save at the start of every chapter.

🗂️ The chapter-save rule

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus (reported by Output Lag and the Vice City Hub). Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Any one-chance feat that goes wrong is then a reload, not a restart.

🚫 Never cheat on this save

Turning on a cheat reportedly disables trophies for the session and blocks saving (PowerPyx, reported by PlanetGTA6 and others). If you want to play with cheats, do it on a separate save file.

⏱️ Honest uncertainty

The game launched on 19 November 2026. Where a method is still unconfirmed, the guide says so and names the source that lists it as unconfirmed, instead of inventing steps. This page will only ever claim what its sources support.
